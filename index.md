## About Me

I received my B.Eng. from the Department of Mechanical and Electronic
Engineering at East China University of Science and Technology, where my
training covered mechanical design and control, and my bachelor's thesis was on
sensor algorithms for mobile robots. I am currently a master's student in Power
Engineering (Engineering Thermophysics) at Shanghai Jiao Tong University, and
my master's thesis is on AI for human thermophysiological modelling. I have
always been interested in the most advanced technologies in the world, and I
keep thinking about how to combine them to build something genuinely
meaningful. Looking ahead, I want to work in depth on AI for healthcare:
medical imaging and generative models, medical foundation models, and robotics
for medical applications.
