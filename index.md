## About Me

I am a master's student in Power Engineering at Shanghai Jiao Tong University,
where my thesis brings machine learning to human thermophysiological
modelling — making physics-based simulations of the human body faster, more
accurate and more personal. I came to this from mechanical engineering: my
undergraduate degree at ECUST was in mechanical and electronic engineering and
my bachelor's thesis dealt with sensing algorithms for mobile robots, a
background that still shapes how I work, in that I reach for a physical model
first and treat learning-based methods as a way to extend it rather than to
replace it. What has stayed constant is an interest in the frontier of
technology and a preference for combining ideas across fields; looking ahead, I
want to work on AI for healthcare — medical imaging and generative models,
medical foundation models, and robotics for clinical applications — where
fast-moving methods can be turned into something that genuinely helps people.
